# Finite-volume heat transfer protocols

Study 45 marches the Graetz equation without axial conduction. Dimensionless transverse coordinate is −1 to 1 and the parabolic mean-normalized velocity is 1.5(1−y²). Temperature is scaled from zero at the walls to one at the inlet. Use 64 and 128 cross-stream cells with implicit axial steps of 0.001 and 0.0005 to dimensionless distance 0.5. Compare against a 512-cell eigenmode reference with a 256-cell reference check. Require bulk temperature error below 0.005, relative Nusselt error below 5% at s ≥ 0.025, exit Nusselt within 0.12 of 7.541, local discrete heat balance below 10⁻⁸ and lower bulk error after refinement.

Study 46 solves steady heat conduction across two 20 mm bonded layers with conductivities 10 and 1 W/(m·K), surface temperatures 320 and 300 K and no contact resistance. Use 20 and 40 cells. Compare a series-resistance face treatment with an arithmetic conductivity diagnostic. Require conserved flux in both and agreement to 10⁻⁷ W/m² and 10⁻⁹ K with the exact series-resistance solution using the physical face treatment.

Study 47 advances one-dimensional transient heat conduction in a 50 mm solid initially at 300 K, with the left face stepped to 320 K and the right face held at 300 K. Use conductivity 10 W/(m·K), diffusivity 10⁻⁵ m²/s, 80 and 160 cells, implicit steps of 0.02 and 0.01 s and an endpoint of 10 s. Require bounded temperature, discrete energy balance below 10⁻⁹ in normalized variables, peak absolute temperature error below 2% of the imposed step and relative hot-wall heat-flux error below 4% at the 0.2 s saved cadence. The fine run must reduce maximum flux error.
