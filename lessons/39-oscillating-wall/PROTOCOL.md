# Study 39 protocol

Periodic channel with 10 mm gap, 8 mm length and 1 mm empty extrusion, initially at rest. Lower wall motion is a 4 Hz sine of amplitude 0.1 m/s, tabulated at the native step. Upper wall is fixed. Viscosity 0.0001 m²/s. OpenFOAM Foundation 14 uses Euler time integration, upwind advection and orthogonal diffusion. Use 320 cells at 1 ms and 640 cells at 0.25 ms for 1 s. Save at 0.01 s intervals after a 0.02 s fine-grid smoke run. The preserved first fine run at 0.5 ms missed the streamwise uniformity check. The revised fine step preserves the coarse case's viscous diffusion number.

Compare every saved cell-average profile with the analytical startup solution. Require RMS below 0.6% of wall speed and maximum absolute error below 2%. Require lower peak RMS on the fine run. Verify expected time steps, linear residuals at most 1e-12, continuity at most 1e-9, velocity bounded by the wall amplitude plus 1e-9 m/s, and transverse velocity and streamwise variation at most 1e-9 m/s. Fit the final period at the wall frequency. Require amplitude error below 2% of wall amplitude and phase error below 0.08 rad where the reference amplitude is at least 10% of the wall amplitude.
