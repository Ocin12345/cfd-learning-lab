# Geometry, physical model and acceptance

The tank is 0.584 x 0.584 m. A released 0.1461 x 0.292 m water column starts at rest. The obstacle spans x=0.292..0.31599948 m and y=0..0.04799896 m. The 92 x 100 logical grid excludes 128 obstacle cells, leaving 9,072 active quads and 9,281 nodes. Cell areas vary: all integrals use actual areas and independently proven native cell ordering.

Water density/dynamic viscosity are 1000 kg/m3 and 0.001 Pa s; air uses 1 kg/m3 and 1.48e-5 Pa s. Gravity is (0,-9.81) m/s2, surface tension 0.07 N/m, wall adhesion disabled. Both phases are incompressible/isothermal; the model is laminar and 2D. Walls are stationary/no slip. The top is static zero gauge pressure and admits air only.

Fluent is planar, double precision, two solver processes, operating density explicitly zero. It uses Coupled pressure/velocity, numerical flow Courant20, explicit pressure/momentum relaxation0.5, explicit Geo-Reconstruct VOF, first-order time advancement, body-force-weighted pressure, second-order-upwind momentum and implicit body force. Full scaled continuity/Ux/Uy criteria are1e-4, with a400-inner cap. Its exact native initial fractions and physical hydrostatic gauge pressure match the analytical declaration.

OpenFOAM Foundation14 uses one0.0146m layer with empty front/back, prghPressure with physical p=0 at the top, pressureInletOutletVelocity and air-only alpha inletOutlet. Native p_rgh is converted using p=p_rgh-rho*9.81*y. Euler time advancement, linear-upwind momentum, interfaceCompression vanLeer1, two alpha correctors, MULES10/tolerance0.01, pressure3 and outer8 are retained. Alpha/pressure linear tolerances remain1e-11/1e-10; velocity linear tolerance1e-6. All final-outer initial Ux/Uy/p_rgh residuals must be<=1e-4. No criterion was relaxed.

The original half-step OpenFOAM parent meets every selected condition through0.25s. Its exact native partitioned checkpoint and51prefix states are byte-verified before continuation. U/UFinal equation relaxation changes from1 to0.7 only after0.25s; all physical properties, mesh, boundaries, dt and numerical limits remain. The continuation computes0.25..0.5s and verifies every suffix step. Prefix/suffix native clocks and fields are checked, not interpolated.

Fluent mass reports are inward-positive; their negative supplies outward flux. OpenFOAM orientedSum(rhoPhi) is outward-positive and divided by the extrusion depth. Euler end-step flux integration closes mixture inventory using real cell areas. At the restart, only one flux per physical step is integrated. Native alpha/U/p fields must be finite, alpha within1e-6 of[0,1], native Courant<=0.25 and absolute relative mixture-mass closure<=0.005. Quiet controls also require Umax<=1e-4 m/s and alpha change<=0.01; Fluent quiet raw targets are1e-12/1e-10/1e-10.

The geometry is adapted from the [Foundation14 dam-break tutorial](https://doc.cfd.direct/openfoam/user-guide-v14/dambreak). The static atmosphere replaces its total-pressure condition. This is not an exact published benchmark replay. No mesh/time independence, turbulence/contact-line accuracy, experimental validation or aircraft applicability is established.
