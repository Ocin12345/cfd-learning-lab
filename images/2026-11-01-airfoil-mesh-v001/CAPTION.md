Figure 5. Actual unstructured triangular mesh for the preserved, closed NACA 0012 polygon at zero incidence. (a) Near-body view; (b) trailing-edge detail. Coordinates are normalized by the 1 m chord. The complete domain contains 383,562 triangles and extends to radius 20 m; only cropped views are shown. Locally prescribed edge sizes resolve the original polygon vertices. Mesh quality and flow accuracy are distinct checks; the image alone establishes neither convergence nor mesh independence. Gmsh generated the mesh.
