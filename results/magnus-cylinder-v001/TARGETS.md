## Physical question and geometry

How does clockwise wall rotation alter the pressure, lift and wake of a circular cylinder in left-to-right flow? This is a viscous laminar startup calculation, not an inviscid analytical validation. NASA describes the qualitative rotating-cylinder lift mechanism: https://www.grc.nasa.gov/www/k-12/airplane/cyl.html . Ansys presents rotating cylinders in its potential-flow teaching: https://innovationspace.ansys.com/courses/courses/simple-approximations-of-fluid-flows/lessons/types-of-potential-flows-lesson-4/ . Those ideal-flow formulas are not numerical acceptance targets for this finite-domain viscous case.
