## Constraints and review focus
- Preserve all original and rejected versions. Fresh version directories only, no installs or publication.
- 9600 shared planar cells, exact constants/boundaries and caps in the spec.
- Native geometry/ordering must be mapped and checked, including the extruded case depth0.01m.
- Fluent absolute/gauge pressure and actual gas constants must be verified; positive fields cannot substitute for convergence.
- OF localEuler indices are not physical time; zero diagonal residuals cannot pass convergence.
- Both root-admitted launches require 4 GiB available RAM/10 GiB free E, 2 ranks, sequential solvers.
- Use saved-state/flux checks to retain real misses; no automated iteration or mesh escalation.
