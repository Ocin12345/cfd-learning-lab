# Offset tandem-square wake: methods

The first square produces a low-speed wake that reaches the offset second square; the final native maps and profiles show interacting downstream velocity deficits. Finite-time lift histories vary during the run; sustained periodicity is not established.

Both codes computed 800 actual steps to 40 s and retained 801 native states. Own primary checks and every numerical miss are retained in the [compact result record](../results/tandem-offset-wake-v001/summary.json).

Two stationary 0.1 m squares sit in a 2 m by 0.75 m planar domain. Their centres are (0.5, -0.025) and (0.9, 0.025) m: the streamwise clear gap is 0.30 m and transverse centre offset is 0.05 m.
